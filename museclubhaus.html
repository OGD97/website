Muse Clubhaus – Visual Identity & Branding

Muse Clubhaus is a comprehensive branding and visual identity project designed to build a strong, modern, and memorable brand presence.

The project centered on designing a distinctive aesthetic and visual system for Muse Clubhaus, combining creative logo marks, harmonious typography, and dynamic graphical assets tailored for modern media.

Creating an identity that stands out while remaining versatile across both digital platforms and physical merchandise, ensuring brand consistency at every customer touchpoint.

Developed a modular identity system featuring bold logomarks, refined color schemes, and flexible layout guidelines to deliver an impactful, recognizable visual identity.

Key Features

  • Custom logo mark and emblem design
  • Cohesive brand colors and typography system
  • Visual guidelines for print and digital assets
  • High-resolution design artboards and mockups
